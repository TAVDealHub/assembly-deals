01 · Executive Summary

Science Beats Incumbents Who Stopped Trying

Nura Teethcare is a UK-based premium oral care brand built on a clinically validated, patent-pending formula: the world's highest concentration dual-hydroxyapatite toothpaste (7% nano-HAP + 5% biomimetic micro-HAP), proven in-vitro to repair enamel over 2× more effectively than fluoride. The product was developed in partnership with Professor Andrei Ionescu at the University of Milan — one of the world's leading researchers in dental biomaterials — and positions Nura as the science-led authority in a category defined by 75 years of stagnation from three dominant incumbents.

Founded in June 2025, Nura scaled to £51k monthly revenue in July 2026 — £700k annualised — built with just £200k of invested capital. Gross margins of 77% on the flagship product and CM3-positive unit economics from the outset signal a fundamentally sound model. This £250k raise, structured as a priced round via ASA, will fund performance marketing scale, inventory unlock, early UK retail entry, and two new product launches. £130k is already committed, with close targeted for end of October 2026. All remaining capacity is EIS eligible with HMRC Advance Assurance confirmed.

02 · Market Context

A £140m+ Category Ripe for Disruption

64% of UK adults have active tooth decay. Yet the product most use to address it — toothpaste — has not meaningfully changed in 75 years. Three incumbents (Colgate, Oral-B, Sensodyne) hold 91% of the UK market with functionally identical fluoride formulations. There is no commercial incentive for them to cannibalise their shelf presence with a genuinely superior product.

The structural shift is now underway. Consumer search behaviour shows a 4× increase in hydroxyapatite-related queries and 3× growth in fluoride-free searches over four years. UK toothpaste spend rose 12% in 2024, driven entirely by premiumisation. The Colgate Max White Ultimate at £24 a tube — on Amazon — signals that incumbents are moving upmarket, but constrained by low-efficacy legacy formulations and trust deficits. Online retail now accounts for 14–20% of UK toothpaste purchases, growing at 6.5–8% CAGR.

03 · Product

The Only 12% Dual-Hydroxyapatite Toothpaste

Nura's flagship Mineralising Paste contains a proprietary dual-HAP system: 7% nano-HAP and 5% biomimetic micro-HAP — the only formulation combining both particle types at this concentration. Developed with and tested by the University of Milan (Professor Andrei Ionescu, Vice-Dean of Dentistry, 100+ academic papers), independent in-vitro testing on human enamel samples showed Nura produces over 2× greater enamel stiffness improvement than the leading fluoride toothpaste (Colgate) after one week of twice-daily brushing. A US patent is pending (application 63/930,907).

The paste retails at £25 per tube — 5–10× the mass-market price point — and converts at 3.9% website CVR, demonstrating strong willingness to pay. Since introducing bundled regimen packs in November 2025, AOV has exceeded £50 inc. VAT.

The full product regimen spans four touchpoints: Mineralising Paste, Biodegradable Floss Picks, Prebiotic Mouthwash Concentrate, and a Hypersoft Manual Toothbrush (German Perlon® filament). This round's proceeds part-fund development of two additional SKUs: a children's toothpaste and a confidential new product in partnership with a high-profile UK dentist.

Scientific credibility note: Lana Fattah, GDC-registered Cosmetic Hygienist at the Harrods Wellness Clinic and one of London's most sought-after hygienists, reviewed Nura independently and now recommends it to patients — including for sensitivity, pre- and post-whitening, and as a fluoride-free alternative for health-conscious patients. This is unsolicited clinical endorsement from within the prestige distribution channel Nura is targeting.

04 · Unfair Advantage

IP, Science, and the Boka Playbook

Patent-Pending IP

The dual-HAP formulation is patent-pending in the US (63/930,907). No competitor currently offers this combination at this concentration. Independent university validation provides credibility that brand-only players cannot manufacture.

University of Milan Partnership

Prof. Ionescu's team conducted the original efficacy studies and continues to act as scientific collaborator. Access to his lab's research pipeline provides ongoing differentiation and publication-backed claims.

ADAI US Distribution

Nura has secured a distribution partnership with American Dental Accessories Inc (ADAI) — the same distributor that launched Boka (>$100m annual Amazon revenue) and Fygg (>$10m ARR). ADAI provides immediate Amazon US access and a network of 25,000+ dental clinics. This is not a speculative US plan; it replicates a proven playbook with proven operators.

Category Builder Network

Bryce Vielguth — former COO and employee #1 at both Boka and Fygg — worked fractionally with Nura and has publicly assessed the team's early metrics as superior to Boka's equivalent stage.

CM3 positivity from first purchase is the key signal: Nura recovers its acquisition cost on the first order, making every repeat purchase incremental margin. The LTV thesis improves materially with each additional order: at 2.65 avg orders the LTV:CAC reaches 4.8×; at 3.95 orders it reaches 7.1×. Honest commentary: The CM3 figure of 13% is meaningful but slim. ROAS has varied from 1.9 (October 2025, inventory disruption) to 3.6 (March 2026), and the blended ROAS since launch is 2.6×. The Feb and Apr 2026 revenue dips (−27% and −17% MoM) were inventory-constrained, not demand-constrained — the team explicitly cut ad spend to avoid stockouts. The story is credible but investors should verify inventory management has been addressed with this raise before committing.

10 · Bull vs. Bear

Honest Assessment

Bull Case

  • Exceptional capital efficiency. £700k ARR from £200k invested. Most DTC consumer brands burn 3–5× this to reach similar revenue, with worse margins.
  • Real product differentiation. 12% dual-HAP, patented, university-validated, and independently endorsed by a senior clinical professional. This is not a branding exercise on commodity ingredients.
  • CM3-positive from day one. The unit economics are structurally sound: acquisition cost recovered on first purchase, subscription upside not yet activated.
  • Category timing. HAP searches up 4×, oral care premiumisation at +12% YoY, incumbents constrained. The window is open and early movers will be hard to displace.
  • Proven US playbook via ADAI. The same distributor that scaled Boka to >$100m Amazon ARR is now Nura's US partner. This is a structural shortcut most early-stage brands don't have.
  • EIS at £4.5m pre. 30% tax relief effectively reduces entry to ~£3.15m pre for eligible investors — a material improvement to risk-adjusted returns.

Bear Case

  • Full entry valuation. £4.5m pre on ~£700k ARR = 6.4× forward ARR. For a 2-founder business pre-subscription and pre-retail, this is a premium. Comparable early consumer raises have often been done at 3–5× ARR.
  • Subscription model unproven. The LTV thesis — LTV:CAC of 4.8–7.1× — relies heavily on subscription launch. The current 35% cohort repeat rate is pre-subscription and encouraging, but LTV projections are aspirational without it.
  • Thin team for the ambition. The 5-year roadmap — UK retail, US DTC, US retail, EU — is being executed by 2 founders with fractional support. No dedicated marketing hire planned in this raise. Founder capacity is the most material constraint on execution.
  • Inventory fragility. Feb and Apr 2026 revenue dips were management-controlled cuts to avoid stockouts — but they reveal a supply chain that cannot yet flex with demand. This is a genuine operational risk at scale.
  • No lead investor. No institutional or lead-angel signal. Increases due diligence burden for individual angels and limits syndicate credibility with future investors.
11 · Analyst Conclusion

Verdict: Back at Small Ticket, EIS Essential

Nura has done something genuinely difficult: it built a product with real scientific IP, validated it independently, priced it at premium, and scaled to meaningful revenue with minimal capital. The unit economics at 14 months in — 68% GM ex. VAT, CM3-positive on first purchase, ROAS 2.5–3.2× — are the kind of numbers that consumer brand investors spend years waiting to see. The Boka comparison is not vanity; it is structural. The same US distributor, the same category dynamic, a potentially superior product on enamel science. Bryce Vielguth — the person who actually built Boka — has said in writing that Nura's early metrics are better.

The risks are real and should be priced in. The entry valuation of £4.5m pre-money is full for the stage: no subscription launched, no retail revenue, no US traction yet, and 2 founders managing the entire operation. The inventory management story requires scrutiny — the Feb/Apr 2026 dips were explained but not resolved. Management's Y2 projection of £2.77m requires 4× growth from today with £250k of new capital, which is ambitious. The absence of a lead investor means this round has no external signal of institutional quality.

For EIS-eligible angels with appetite for early-stage consumer risk, the effective entry at ~£3.15m pre (after 30% EIS relief) and the structural quality of the product and economics make this worth a small ticket. Investors should request the 18-month MoM cashflow model, probe the subscription launch timeline and inventory management plan, and understand the path to Series A before committing. This is not a safe bet — it is a well-backed early-stage consumer play in a category with generational tailwinds.
